The 1,450 figure describes the initial project, not current daily capacity. No new certifications, customer testimonials or performance statistics have been invented.

Published contact: info@avrocompany.com; +964 751 403 5711. Erbil: Empire Business Tower, T4, 20th floor, units 3, 4, 5 & 6. Duhok: Qazi Mohammad Road. Registration 1676.

## Original bilingual branding

Replaced the generic text wordmarks with transparent adaptations of the supplied English and Kurdish logos, across the header, mountain opening and footer. Lettering contours and the golden sun are based on the originals. Native navy-and-gold PNG assets remain available; an SVG color filter renders light ink and gold on dark website backgrounds. The Kurdish version is also the favicon.

Selected files: `dist/assets/kurda-logo-english.png` and `dist/assets/kurda-logo-kurdish.png`.

Image editing used the built-in image-generation tool. Final prompt set: remove only the white background, retain the original navy lettering/calligraphy and golden sun, preserve their shape and proportions, produce a transparent PNG with clean solid shapes and no added elements. The Kurdish edit used the larger original from the existing website as the explicit edit target.
