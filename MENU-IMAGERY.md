# Visual menu artwork

Category artwork is representative, not photography of a served dish. No ingredients, dietary claims or availability are inferred from illustrations. Images use `object-fit: contain` to preserve proportions.

## Font

Noto Sans Arabic supports Arabic and Sorani Kurdish. It is self-hosted and subset from the variable font in Google's official font repository: https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/notosansarabic. The SIL Open Font License is included at `dist/menu/assets/Noto-Sans-Arabic-OFL.txt`. DM Sans remains the Latin font. No remote font requests are made by visitors.
